Run the plugin studio loop:

1. traction skill: collect and report for studio.json (create it with the
   portfolio skill if missing).
2. decide skill: a memo for every plugin at day 30 or later without one yet.
3. radar skill: rank candidates.md plus open `request` issues from the
   portfolio.
4. Finish with a one-screen summary: what moved, what was decided, and the
   top three next builds with their hero workflows. Offer to start the top one
   with `/plugin-creator:new`. Do not publish or post anything.
